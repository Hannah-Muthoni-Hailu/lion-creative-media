The production workflow

From first enquiry to final delivery

Every project follows the same ten steps, so you know what is happening, what is being delivered, when it will arrive and what we need from you.

  1. EnquiryWe respond within one business day.
  2. DiscoveryWe understand your brief and budget.
  3. QuotationSent within 24–48 hours once we have enough information.
  4. BookingSigned approval and a deposit confirm your date.
  5. Pre-productionAn approved concept, schedule and shot list.
  6. ProductionWe shoot according to the agreed scope.
  7. EditingYour first draft arrives within the agreed timeline.
  8. RevisionsFeedback is tracked across the agreed revision rounds.
  9. DeliveryOrganised files are handed over on the agreed date, with final payment.
  10. Follow-upWe ask for your feedback and talk about what comes next.
