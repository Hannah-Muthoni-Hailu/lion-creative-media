Meet Joseph Kyuli — Behind the Lens

Joseph Kyuli founded Lion Creative Media House to give people and brands a visual identity with real presence. He leads every project with a careful eye for light, emotion and detail, from the first conversation to the final frame. Based in Nairobi and available for assignments outside the city, he brings photography, film and post-production together so clients get one creative team from idea to delivery.
