### 1. Wear something you already love

Pick an outfit you feel good in, not one you are saving for the camera. Comfortable clothes show in your posture and your expression.

### 2. Tell us how you want to feel

Before the session, share the mood you are after: confident, warm, bold or quiet. It helps us choose the light, the setting and the pace.

### 3. Move instead of freezing

Stiff poses read as stiff photos. Shift your weight, turn your shoulders, and let your hands rest naturally. Small movements keep your face and body loose.

### 4. Breathe and take your time

A slow breath between frames resets your shoulders and your smile. Nobody is rushing, and the best frames often come after the first few minutes of warming up.

### 5. Trust your photographer

Good direction should feel like a conversation. Ask questions, say what you like, and let us guide the details so you can focus on being yourself.
