Choosing a wedding photographer in Nairobi is one of the biggest decisions in your planning. The cake is eaten and the flowers fade, but the photographs stay with you for life. A little care at the booking stage saves a lot of worry later.

## Start with the style, not the price

Look at full galleries, not only the best six images on a website. Do you like the light, the colours and the way people look when they are relaxed? Some photographers favour bright, airy images, others prefer deep colour and drama. Pick the style that feels like you.

## Ask what is included

Packages differ widely, so compare like with like. Ask:

- How many hours of coverage are included?
- Will there be one photographer or a team?
- Is a second photographer or a film add-on available?
- How many edited photos will you receive, and in what format?

## Ask about timing and delivery

A good photographer will tell you when to expect your first sneak peek and your full gallery. Ask how the files are delivered and how long they are kept online. Put the dates in writing.

## Meet before you book

You will spend most of the day with this person, so you should feel comfortable with them. A short call or coffee tells you more than any portfolio. Talk through your venue, your family groups and any moments you cannot afford to miss.

## Plan the day together

The best wedding photos come from a realistic timeline. Leave room for portraits, family groups and the unplanned moments in between. Share your schedule early, and tell your photographer about anything sensitive, such as family situations, so they can handle it with care.

## Get it all in writing

Confirm the date, the hours, the deliverables, the deposit and the balance in a signed agreement. It protects both of you and keeps everyone clear on what to expect.
