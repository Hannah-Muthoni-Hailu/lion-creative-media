About Us

Lion Creative Media House is a Nairobi-based commercial photography, film and creative production studio. We help businesses communicate their value through powerful visuals, professional production and purposeful storytelling.

Meet Joseph Kyuli

Joseph Kyuli founded Lion Creative Media House to give people and brands a visual identity with real presence. He leads every project with a careful eye for light, emotion and detail, from the first conversation to the final frame.

Based in Nairobi and available for assignments outside the city, the studio brings photography, film and post-production together so every project has one team behind it, from the first idea to the final file.
