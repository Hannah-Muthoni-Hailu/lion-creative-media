ABOUT LION CREATIVE MEDIA HOUSE

Meet Joseph Kyuli

Joseph Kyuli founded Lion Creative Media House to give people and brands a visual identity with real presence. He leads every project with a careful eye for light, emotion and detail, from the first conversation to the final frame.

Based in Nairobi and available for assignments outside the city, the studio brings photography, film, editing and branding together, so clients get a single creative team from idea to delivery.
